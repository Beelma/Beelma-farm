01Our story

Built for better beef

Good beef
starts with care.

At Beelma Farms Feedlot, we believe good beef starts with good management.

We are a Zambian-owned feedlot based in Mazabuka, dedicated to raising healthy, well-cared-for cattle through clean facilities, proper nutrition, and daily monitoring. Our mission is simple: to produce quality, traceable beef that Zambians can trust, while contributing to food security and the growth of local agriculture.

From clean water and spacious pens to proper feeding and veterinary care, we do the small things right because we know they lead to big results. This is more than farming. This is our contribution to feeding the nation.

Our Mission: To raise quality cattle through ethical, professional feedlot management.

Our Vision: To become a trusted name in local and international beef, known for quality, consistency, and care.
